01 / The idea

An additional access point.

Echo Frame asks how sign-language animation might become part of watching online video. The project is currently presented as a concept; sign-language accuracy and real-time performance require further development and validation.

01

The context

Online video often assumes access to sound. The concept focuses on a visual language channel within the existing viewing experience.

02

The proposal

A browser extension would receive audio and present sign-language animation alongside video, giving the viewer control over an additional visual layer.

03

The next design questions

Language coverage, translation accuracy, timing and evaluation with sign-language users are open questions. No validated interpretation quality is claimed.
