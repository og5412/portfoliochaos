This project is in development. The page presents its current direction and open design questions.

01 / The idea

Designing around care and trust.

Still Here considers how a message might be preserved without losing the sender’s control. The current concept explores inactivity signals, trusted contacts and timed verification as parts of a carefully considered release process.

01

Create a private space

Messages are prepared ahead of time for an intended recipient. The experience centres on privacy, clarity and the emotional weight of the content.

02

Check before release

Inactivity, trusted contacts and timed verification are proposed checkpoints. They are concept directions, rather than a guarantee that the system can determine someone’s circumstances.

03

Keep control visible

The central design questions are how to pause, edit, withdraw and verify a message. These decisions need further development before a delivery service could be responsibly offered.
